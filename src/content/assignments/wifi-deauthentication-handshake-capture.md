Moving from passive monitoring to active packet injection with a targeted deauthentication attack. Using airodump-ng to map the network (BSSID, channel) and aireplay-ng to inject forged management frames, the target is forced to disconnect and, on reconnection, its WPA/WPA2 authentication data (4-Way Handshake / EAPOL) is captured, a preparatory stage for full network compromise.
