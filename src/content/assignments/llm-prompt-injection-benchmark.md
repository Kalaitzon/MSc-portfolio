A benchmark for evaluating and hardening Large Language Models against prompt injection. A realistic bank-support assistant with canary tokens auto-detects data leaks across 60 benign and adversarial tests in five attack families; a three-layer defence (input check, context separation, output filtering) is validated by ablation, driving attack success to zero, with an LLM-judge and cross-model comparison (Llama 3.1 8B vs Mistral 7B).
