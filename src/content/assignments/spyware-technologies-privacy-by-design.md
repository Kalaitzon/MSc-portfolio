A technical study of the evolution of spyware such as Pegasus and Predator into state-level cyberweapons, centred on the FORCEDENTRY zero-click attack against iMessage and the limits of perimeter defence and anti-forensics. Proposes a shift to Privacy by Design (ISO/IEC 29100, 27701) and the Zero Trust architectural design of an Electronic Health Record using RBAC and Privacy-Enhancing Technologies (PETs).
