A comparative security analysis of WPA2 and WPA3 on Kali Linux. A deauthentication attack on WPA2 captures the 4-Way Handshake for an offline dictionary attack, while WPA3's protections (Protected Management Frames, SAE/Dragonfly) are evaluated. Using Airgeddon, an Evil Twin exploits WPA3 Transition Mode to show how downgrade attacks can force devices onto older, vulnerable standards.
