Analysis of IEEE 802.11 management frames (Beacon, Probe) and the 4-way handshake (RSNA) in Wireshark, followed by an active Layer-2 Evil Twin attack. By manually creating a rogue access point, the work demonstrates the protocol's inherent lack of AP authentication, which allows client devices to be successfully deceived.
