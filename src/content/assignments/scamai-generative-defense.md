A group project presenting ScamAI, which combines Generative AI and Machine Learning against phishing/scamming. It works in two stages: passive detection of malicious messages via a Random Forest trained on 13,919 emails, and active scambaiting using LLM personas tailored to the scam type. Emphasises Responsible AI with output guardrails and prompt-injection defences, plus an ethical/legal analysis (GDPR, AI Act, NIS2).
