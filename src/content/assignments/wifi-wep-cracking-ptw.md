Practical compromise of the WEP protocol, exposing its weaknesses in IV management and the RC4 cipher. A controlled testbed is built on Kali Linux with airbase-ng as a virtual access point; captured packets feed aircrack-ng using the PTW method, recovering the full 64-bit key from only ~15,000 IVs and proving WEP's inadequacy for modern networks.
