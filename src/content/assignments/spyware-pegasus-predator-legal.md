A legal and technical study of intrusive spyware such as Pegasus and Predator through the lens of digital constitutionalism. Examines zero-click infection mechanisms and the national and European framework on lifting communications privacy, and — through the Pegasus and Greek 'Predatorgate' case studies — argues (de lege ferenda) for a risk-based regulatory approach aligned with the EU AI Act.
