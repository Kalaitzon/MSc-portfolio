A risk-adaptive access-control engine built on Zero Trust, combining Machine Learning with deterministic rules. On synthetic enterprise logs simulating normal activity and attacks (Impossible Travel, Privilege Escalation, Bulk Data Spike), a hybrid of Gradient Boosting and Isolation Forest with hard safety rules reaches PR-AUC 0.942, and adds Responsible-AI explainability, fairness analysis and concept-drift simulation.
