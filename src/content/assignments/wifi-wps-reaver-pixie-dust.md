Analysis of the architectural vulnerabilities of Wi-Fi Protected Setup (WPS) on WPA2-PSK networks. Covers deauthentication to capture the 4-way handshake for an offline dictionary attack, brute-force with Reaver, and the Pixie-Dust technique exploiting weak nonce generation on certain chipsets, while showing how modern WPS Lockout defences block automated attempts.
